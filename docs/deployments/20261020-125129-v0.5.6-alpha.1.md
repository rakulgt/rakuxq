## Contract

`max_candidates` missing, null, empty, auto or 1 means primary only. Explicit 2 enables a
quality-filtered alternative. Numeric strings are supported; invalid counts, booleans and
fractions return 422 before search. The primary time defaults to 3000 ms. Single search is capped
at 9000 ms; paired search at 8000 ms primary plus at most 1000 ms verification (short requests
reserve one third of primary time). The old 50–10000-ms input range remains compatible, with
effective caps disclosed. No depth/pro control was added.

All engine entrypoints share this policy. FEN solutions keep `time_ms`; fused image solutions and
low-level/Lab analysis keep `movetime_ms`. Existing primary fields, display text and frozen-primary
quality safeguards remain. Search budgets exclude upload, vision, queueing, transport and exceptional
protocol/scheduling overhead. Two is a maximum, not a guarantee of a useful second move.

Lab adds recommendation-count and 6/9-second time choices. Its proxy read/send wait is raised from
8 to 45 seconds to avoid truncating a valid 9-second search; search time is not increased to 45s.
One engine/worker, serial execution, authentication/allowlist, free on-site Lab, licensing, pricing,
models, network and retention remain unchanged. No new database schema migration is required.
