## Changes and constraints

One serialized MultiPV search uses the existing total time budget, with a default of 3000 ms.
Qualified alternatives are exposed in `recommendations` and existing `display_text`; `best_move`
and automatic play remain primary-only. Lab shows both sibling choices, not two consecutive plies.
The anonymous homepage recommendation column is unchanged and remains primary-only.

Only distinct legal roots with comparable same-depth exact scores are accepted. The default
non-mate gap is 150 engine points; a second line that drops a known winning mate or introduces a
forced loss is suppressed. Uncertain/bound-only comparisons produce one move with an explicit
omission reason. At an interrupted final iteration, a matching completed pair may be used at most
one ply below the primary's latest reported depth. No extra search is started just to fill the list.

The engine binary/network, key allowlist, pricing, identity boundary, visual model, log retention
and database schema were not changed. Single-thread/64-MiB/one-worker limits remain in effect.
There is no move-history input in this change; perpetual-check/chase adjudication is not solved.
