## Changes

The primary receives a full default 3000-ms single-PV search and is frozen before an optional
verification stage of at most 1000 ms. Both stages reuse the hash under the same engine lock.
One monotonic deadline and protocol headroom bound the combined search budget to 4000 ms.
Short primary budgets reserve one-third as much optional time. Unique legal moves, terminal
positions and insufficient remaining budget skip the optional stage. Its failure cannot discard
the primary answer. No extra engine process or worker is introduced.

The short stage compares legal roots at the same completed depth, including the frozen primary;
the existing quality threshold and an additional conservative cross-stage check apply. Different
stage depths/precision remain explicit. The second stage cannot overwrite the primary move,
score, depth, nodes, nps or PV; total time and stage metadata are reported separately. Existing
`best_move` and `display_text` clients remain supported. Legacy larger inputs are accepted within
the original validation range but effective primary time is capped at 3000 ms and disclosed.

This replaces the previous shared three-second MultiPV strategy. It is not a streamed response,
an Elo guarantee or a history-aware perpetual-check/chase adjudicator. Upload, vision, queueing,
transport and exceptional scheduling/fault overhead are outside a hard end-to-end time guarantee.
The engine/network pair, visual model, authentication, pricing, retention and database schema
are unchanged. No secret, user image or raw audit log is included in source or release assets.
