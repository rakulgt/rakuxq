## Incident and behavior

The retained audit window contained 63 fused requests: 60 successful responses and three HTTP 502
responses for one repeated position. Pikafish rejected the supplied mover because the non-moving
king was in check. These were not search timeouts or proofs that the opponent had no legal move.
The old fused handler discarded the completed recognition when forwarding the engine error.

The new UCI preflight reports `SIDE_TO_MOVE_CONFLICT` before sending such a position to Pikafish.
It preserves explicit/auto mover semantics and does not silently swap sides. Valid check evasions,
including a single legal move and forced-mate positions, remain analyzable. Zero legal moves is
reported explicitly as checkmate or Xiangqi stalemate, including the winner.

After successful image recognition, fused analysis errors retain the FEN, complete recognition,
readable text, independent analysis status, and error code. The response is HTTP 200 with business
status `review_required` for invalid positions or `partial_success` for runtime analysis failures.
FEN-only error responses and all existing authentication/engine allowlists remain in place.
Short-lived audits record recognition and analysis outcomes separately.
